## 📖 Project Overview
**Track:** Lifestyle & Personal Productivity
**Problem Statement:** Stress & Workload Manager (Preventing University Student Burnout)

University students juggle immense cumulative loads across five core pillars of life (Mental, Time, Physical, Social, Errands). Traditional time-management tools merely track time, treating all tasks equally and waiting until the student crashes to reflect the overload. **Buffer** is a load-balancing mobile app that shifts from passive tracking to active capacity management. Utilizing a frictionless, low-stress "Sketch Journal" aesthetic, Buffer tracks daily capacity. When a user's load breaches 90%, an AI-driven "Triage Mode" automatically intervenes to categorize and delay non-urgent tasks, protecting the student's baseline mental health without adding the cognitive burden of rescheduling.
